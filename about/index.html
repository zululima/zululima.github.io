About

I'm a software developer at Taito United Oy, where my work is consulting. I have a master's degree from the University of Helsinki, with software engineering as my major.

My thesis gathered 594 unique public software licenses from five license lists, and it recommends AGPL-3.0-or-later where possible. I also build on Nostr and Bitcoin: Mismeet, an app I'm making, shares locations between friends end-to-end encrypted.

The hacker culture of the 70's greatly inspires me.

The blog is plain HTML and CSS on GitHub Pages.
