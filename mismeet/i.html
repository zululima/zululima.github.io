Mismeet invite

Someone sent you this link to add them as a contact in Mismeet, an app that shares locations between phones over Nostr, end-to-end encrypted.

  1. Install Mismeet.
    App Store Google Play
  2. Open the link you were sent again. With Mismeet installed, it opens the app on Add contact with the invite filled in.
  3. Or copy the invite and paste it in Mismeet under Contacts, then Add.

The invite sits after the # in the address, which browsers never send to a server, so this site does not see it.

This link carries no invite. Ask the person who sent it for a new one.
